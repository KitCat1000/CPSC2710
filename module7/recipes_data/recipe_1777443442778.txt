Yakisoba|||Japanese|||23|||10|||2|||* 4 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 

* 2 tsp soy sauce

* 4 tsp oyster sauce

* 4 tsp ketchup

* 2 tsp sugar

* Noodles: 1 lb of fresh, pre-cooked mushi-chukamen

* Protein: ½ lb of thinly sliced pork belly or chicken thighs.

* Vegetables: ½ a small cabbage (chopped), 1 carrot (julienned), and ½ an onion (sliced).

* Garnishes: Beni shoga, aonori, and katsuobushi.
|||1. Prep the Noodles: If using packaged fresh noodles, rinse them under hot water to loosen the strands and drain thoroughly.

2. Sear the Meat: Heat a large wok or skillet over high heat with a tablespoon of neutral oil. Cook the meat until browned and crispy, then remove and set aside.

3. Stir-fry Vegetables: In the same pan, add the onion, carrot, and cabbage. Sauté for 2–3 minutes until the cabbage is slightly tender but still has a crunch.

4. Combine & Sauce: Add the noodles and cooked meat back into the pan. Pour the sauce over everything and toss vigorously for 2–3 minutes to coat evenly and let the sauce caramelize slightly.
