Ginger Chicken Stir-Fry|||Chinese|||15|||15|||2|||Chicken: 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized chunks.

Aromatics: 2 inches of fresh ginger (thinly sliced), 4 scallions (cut into 2” pieces), 1 chili pepper (optional).

Marinade: 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tbsp light soy sauce, pinch of salt, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1.5 tsp cornstarch.

Sauce: 1/4 cup chicken stock, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp light soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp cornstarch.

Finishing: 1 tbsp peanut or vegetable oil (for frying), 1 tsp toasted sesame oil. 
|||Marinate the Chicken: Combine chicken with Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, salt, and grated ginger. Mix until absorbed, then toss with cornstarch. Let sit for 10–15 minutes.

Prep the Sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together all the sauce ingredients.

Sear the Ginger: Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add sliced ginger and cook until the edges are slightly charred and fragrant.

Cook the Chicken: Spread chicken in the wok (don't overlap too much). Let sear for 1–2 minutes until browned on the bottom, then flip and brown the other side.

Stir-Fry & Sauce: Add scallions and chili pepper, stir-frying for 30 seconds. Pour in the sauce and stir constantly for about 30 seconds until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken.
