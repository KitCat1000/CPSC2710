cayenne pepper to taste|||Brown the Meat: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken pieces for 5 minutes, then remove. Add the sliced sausage and sauté until browned; remove and set aside with the chicken.

Sauté the Trinity: In the same pot, add the onion, celery, and bell pepper. Sauté for 5–7 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

Toast Rice & Spices: Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the rice and all dry seasonings (Cajun spice, oregano, thyme, bay leaves). Stir for 1–2 minutes to toast the rice.

Simmer: Pour in the crushed tomatoes and chicken broth. Return the chicken and sausage to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and cover. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until the rice is tender.

Add Shrimp: In the last 5 minutes of cooking, gently fold in the raw shrimp. Cover and cook until they are pink and opaque.
