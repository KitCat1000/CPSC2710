Tacos|||Other|||5|||10|||4|||Meat: 1 lb lean ground beef (85/15 is recommended for flavor).

Seasoning: 1 packet (1 oz) taco seasoning OR a mix of chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, and oregano.

Liquid: 1/2 cup water and 2 tbsp tomato paste (for a saucy consistency).

Shells: 12 hard taco shells or small flour/corn tortillas.

Standard Toppings: Shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, shredded cheddar cheese, and sour cream

Optional: Limes to taste|||Brown the Meat: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the ground beef until no pink remains (about 5–7 minutes). Drain any excess fat.

Add Flavor: Stir in the taco seasoning, tomato paste, and water. Bring to a simmer and cook for 2–5 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the meat.

Warm the Shells:
Hard Shells: Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 5 minutes to make them extra crispy.

Soft Tortillas: Heat in a dry skillet for 15 seconds per side to make them pliable and more flavorful.
