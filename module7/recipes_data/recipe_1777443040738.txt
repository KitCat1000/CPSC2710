Blueberry Cake|||Desserts|||60|||45|||10|||Blueberries: 1 ½ cups fresh blueberries (toss with 1 tbsp flour to prevent sinking).
1 ½ cups all-purpose flour, 
1 tsp baking powder 
¼ tsp salt.
½ cup unsalted butter (softened)
¾ cup white sugar (divided)
1 tsp vanilla extract
2 large eggs (separated)
⅓ cup milk.|||Prep: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8-inch square or round baking pan.

Cream Butter: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and ½ cup of the sugar until light and fluffy.

Mix in the vanilla and salt, then add the egg yolks one at a time, beating until creamy.

Mix Batter: Whisk the flour and baking powder together. Add this to the butter mixture alternately with the milk, starting and ending with the flour.

Add Berries: Gently fold in the floured blueberries.

Fold Egg Whites: In a separate clean bowl, beat the egg whites until soft peaks form. Gradually add the remaining ¼ cup of sugar, beating until stiff peaks form. Gently fold these whites into the cake batter to keep it light.

Bake: Pour the batter into the pan and sprinkle the top with an extra tablespoon of sugar for a crunchy finish. Bake for 45–50 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
