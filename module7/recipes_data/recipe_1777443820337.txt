Coq au Vin|||French|||20|||30|||4|||Chicken: 3-4 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs and legs.

Aromatics: 1 large yellow onion (diced), 3-4 carrots (sliced), and 4-6 cloves of garlic (minced).

Braising Liquid: 1 bottle (750ml) dry red wine (Pinot Noir or Burgundy) and 1-2 cups chicken stock.

Flavor Boosters: 4-8 oz thick-cut bacon or lardons, 2 tbsp tomato paste, and optional ¼ cup Cognac or brandy for deglazing.

Herbs: A bouquet garni of fresh thyme, bay leaves, and parsley.

Finish: 8-10 oz cremini mushrooms (quartered), 8 oz pearl onions (peeled), and 2-4 tbsp butter|||Marinate (Optional but Recommended): For deeper flavor, marinate the chicken with the wine, sliced onions, and carrots for at least 4 hours or overnight in the refrigerator.

Render Bacon: In a large Dutch oven, cook the diced bacon until crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside, keeping the fat in the pot.

Brown Chicken: Pat the chicken dry, season with salt and pepper, and brown in the bacon fat over medium-high heat (about 5-8 minutes per side). Remove and set aside.

Sauté Veggies: Cook the onions and carrots in the same pot until softened. Add garlic and tomato paste, stirring for 1 minute.

Deglaze and Simmer: Add Cognac (if using) and the wine marinade, scraping the bottom of the pot for browned bits. Return the chicken and bacon to the pot, add the chicken stock and herbs, and bring to a simmer.

Cook: Cover and simmer on the stovetop or bake in a 325°F (160°C) oven for about 45 minutes to 1 hour, until the chicken is tender.

Final Garnish: While the chicken cooks, sauté the mushrooms and pearl onions in butter until golden. Add them to the stew in the last 10-15 minutes of cooking.

Thicken Sauce: If the sauce is too thin, remove the chicken and simmer the liquid until reduced, or whisk in a beurre manié (equal parts softened butter and flour).
