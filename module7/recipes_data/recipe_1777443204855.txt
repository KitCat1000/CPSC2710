Schnitzel|||German|||45|||30|||4|||* Meat: 4 boneless pork chops or veal cutlets (approx. 1 lb).

* Dredge: ½ cup all-purpose flour.

* Binder: 2 large eggs, lightly beaten.

* Breading: ¾ to 1 cup plain or panko breadcrumbs.

* Seasoning: Salt, freshly ground black pepper, and optionally a pinch of paprika or nutmeg.

* Frying: Neutral oil (like canola or vegetable) or lard.

* Garnish: Fresh lemon wedges and parsley.|||1. Pound the Meat: Place each cutlet between two sheets of plastic wrap. Use the flat side of a meat mallet to pound them to an even thickness of approximately ¼ inch. This ensures the meat cooks quickly and stays tender.

2. Season: Lightly sprinkle both sides of each cutlet with salt and pepper.

3. Set Up Breading Station: Arrange three shallow bowls in a line:

    * Bowl 1: Flour.
    * Bowl 2: Whisked eggs.
    * Bowl 3: Breadcrumbs.

4. Bread the Cutlets:
    * Coat each piece in flour and shake off any excess.
    * Dip into the egg wash, ensuring it’s fully covered.
    * Transfer to the breadcrumbs. Pro Tip: Do not press the crumbs into the meat; a light coating helps the crust "puff" and stay airy during frying.

5. Shallow Fry: Heat about ¼ to ½ inch of oil in a large skillet to 330°F–350°F. Carefully add the schnitzels (do not overcrowd the pan).

6. Cook and Serve: Fry for 2–3 minutes per side until they are deep golden brown. Drain on a paper towel-lined plate and serve immediately with a squeeze of fresh lemon
